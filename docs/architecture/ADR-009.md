# ADR-009: Job identity, deduplication and lifecycle

**Status:** Accepted (Phase 5)

## Context
Discovery re-reads the same boards every few hours. Jobs must not be duplicated, must
show when they were first and last seen, and must disappear when the posting is closed,
without false expiries from a partial or failed response. The same job can also appear
on two platforms when an employer migrates or cross-posts.

## Decision
Identity and dedup (`agents/agent_3_deduplication.py`, pure functions) apply three
layers in order to every posting of a crawled board:
1. **Same company row + board job id** (`external_id`): update it if its **content
   hash** changed (SHA-256 of the normalized job excluding `raw`); otherwise only mark it
   seen. Repeats within one response are skipped.
2. **Same URL as a different job** (any company): skipped, because URLs are unique in
   the database.
3. **Same fingerprint** (normalized company + title + location, as `dedup_key`) as an
   active job of a *different* company row: skipped as a cross-platform duplicate.
   Identical titles within one company are kept, since employers post several openings.

Lifecycle (`services/discovery_service.py`, `repositories/job_repository.py`):
- `first_seen_at` is set on insert and never changes; `last_seen_at` advances on every
  crawl that sees the job.
- After a crawl, jobs of that company not seen in it become `expired` (`expired_at`
  set), **only if the response was complete**, and never for jobs in `applied` (or
  already `expired`).
- An expired job that reappears is revived (`status='new'`, `expired_at` cleared)
  instead of being inserted again.
- Each company is persisted in one short transaction. Invalid postings are counted and
  sampled, never stored.

Every run is recorded in `discovery_runs`:
- counts for found, inserted, updated, unchanged, skipped and expired
- per-company details: status, error, skip reasons, invalid samples, notes

Companies keep `last_crawl_status`, `last_error` and `consecutive_failures`.

## Consequences
- Re-running discovery is idempotent; unchanged jobs cost only a `last_seen_at` update.
- History and applied jobs are never lost to expiry, and truncated responses cannot mass-expire.
- A fingerprint that includes location means one role with differently written locations
  on two platforms is kept twice (accepted for now).
- `tests/unit/test_deduplication.py` and `tests/integration/test_discovery.py` cover
  every branch.
